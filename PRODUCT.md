# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript

## Users

Fans of Avicii, music lovers, and first-time visitors who want to quickly understand his impact and experience an interactive tribute moment.

## Product Purpose

Create a memorial fan page that honors Avicii with a respectful tribute tone while still reflecting the energy of his music through visuals and interaction.

## Positioning

A tribute experience that blends classical memorial character with modern DJ-stage interaction, not just a static biography page.

## Operating Context

Single-page website, mobile and desktop, consumed in browsers as a lightweight public page (including GitHub Pages style hosting).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must include Avicii name and logo treatment.
- Must include a strong hero image, short introduction, short bio, interesting facts, and one interactive DJ wheel.
- Interactive wheel should light up and play short tribute audio hooks inspired by famous songs.
- No frameworks (React/Vue/etc.); plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only.

## Brand Commitments

- Tribute-first tone acknowledging Avicii's passing.
- Theme direction: classical touch fused with modern DJ music energy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing repo structure for a static site with `index.html`.
- User-provided creative brief and constraints.
- No licensed source package of official brand assets was provided in-repo.

## Product Principles

1. Honor legacy without flattening energy.
2. Pair emotional storytelling with musical interaction.
3. Keep the experience elegant, fast, and accessible in a single static page.
4. Prefer authenticity over gimmicks in copy and interaction.
