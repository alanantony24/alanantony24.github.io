DJ Bio

Born in Stockholm, Tim Bergling rose from a young bedroom producer to a globally defining EDM DJ. His sets and productions blended uplifting melodies with festival power, helping tracks like Levels, Wake Me Up, and The Nights become generation-defining anthems. Though he passed in 2018, his sound remains a blueprint for modern dance music.

DJ Legacy Highlights

Signature Melodic House Sound

Avicii helped define melodic EDM by balancing emotional toplines with powerful, crowd-ready drops.

Mainstage Era Icon

He became one of the era's most recognized festival DJs, shaping the sound of global mainstage culture.

Legacy Beyond the Stage

His music still drives new producers, and his legacy continues through fans, artists, and the Tim Bergling Foundation.
