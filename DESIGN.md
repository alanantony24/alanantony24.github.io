# Design

## Visual world

A memorial-stage aesthetic: midnight backgrounds, subtle light bloom, gold ceremonial accents, and electric cyan performance accents to combine reverence with EDM energy.

## Typography

- **Display:** Cinzel (classical authority and tribute tone)
- **Body/UI:** Manrope (modern clarity and digital rhythm)

## Color roles

- Background: deep navy/black
- Primary text: cool off-white
- Tribute accent: warm gold
- Energy accent: electric cyan

## Composition

- Hero split layout (copy + portrait)
- Story and facts as supporting chapters
- Large interactive circular DJ wheel as the experiential centerpiece

## Motion and interaction

- Ambient aurora drift background
- Interactive wheel pads with light pulse state
- Web Audio synthesized tribute riffs for each track button

## Tone rules

- Respectful, non-sensational memorial voice
- Avoid clutter and gimmicky neon overload
- Keep interaction meaningful and tied to Avicii's musical legacy
